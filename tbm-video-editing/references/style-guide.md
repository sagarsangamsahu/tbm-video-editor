# Style guide: what works on Reels and Shorts

Status: general principles. Replace these with specifics once real reference reels are studied (ask the user for Instagram links to 3 to 5 reels they love, then note font, caption position, cut rhythm, zoom timing, B-roll use and music).

## Podcast clips (Raj Shamani / Ranveer Allahbadia style)
- First 1 to 2 seconds: the strongest line. Use a cold open if the clip builds slowly.
- Tight framing on the speaker, with the eye line fixed; punch-in zooms on emphasis and at every cut.
- Big keyword captions in the lower third, never over the face.
- Host reactions are cut. Host questions stay only if the answer can't stand alone.
- Music, if any, is a low bed (about -25 dB under the voice) and never fights the speech.

## Solo talking-head (Matt Gray style)
- Dead air and filler removed (`tighten_gaps` 0.5 to 0.6); retakes cut.
- A framing change (base ↔ punch-in) every few seconds keeps attention.
- One idea per video, delivered as a list or framework when possible.
- Clean background and simple bold captions; let the words carry the video.

## Example brand: Sagar Sangam Sahu (example only, every student picks their own)
- Keyword: Impact, lime `#C6F25E`. Context line: Inter SemiBold, white, lowercase.
- CTA end card: "register for the" / "ORGANIC SYSTEM|WORKSHOP" with a lime arrow.
- Speed 1.1x.

## Reference Sagar loves (Oct 30 2026): instagram.com/p/Dc7_blITEAh
Measured: 88 s, 154 wpm (calm, unhurried), 0.7 cuts per 10 s, -14.6 LUFS, quiet music bed.
- **Headline = a quoted objection, as if a viewer asked it**: `"WHY WOULD / ANYONE PAY ₹4 LAKH?"`. Uppercase, heavy condensed font, quotation marks, line 1 white, line 2 in the accent colour (red there; lime for Sagar). It stays on screen the whole reel, in a dark band above the video.
- The dark top and bottom bands **fade softly into the video** (gradient), no hard edge.
- **Captions are quiet**: 1 to 3 words, white, small, one line, low on the video. No big keyword word. The headline does the shouting.
- **Proof B-roll every 5 to 10 s, all real**: handwriting the number on paper ("4 Lac"), phone showing a client's Instagram profile, screenshots of results, a grid of client thumbnails with view counts, event and team photos, office work.
- Talking head is warm, full-bleed in its band, shallow depth of field; hands and expressions carry the energy.
- Ends on a soft CTA ("come in to DM").
