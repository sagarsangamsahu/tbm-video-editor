# Pro standards (read once per project)

Collected from top editors' house styles and from real feedback. Keep what fits the speaker's values; drop anything that doesn't.

## 1. References: copy or inspiration (ask at intake)
- **Inspiration (default):** learn the style (fonts, colours, placement, pacing, sound logic) and design ORIGINAL captions and graphics for THIS speaker's words.
- **Close copy (only if they say so):** match the reference's layout and timing as closely as possible, but always with this speaker's own words, numbers and footage. Never reuse someone else's footage, logo or text.
- Studying a reference: make timestamped frame sheets (4-8 fps for short clips, 1 fps for long), zoom into moving elements, sample colours, note where text sits and how long it stays. Write what you learned into `references/style-guide.md`.

## 2. Colour theme (auto, when the user has no brand colour)
- Sample the speaker's outfit (chest area below the face) and the background on 6-10 frames.
- Accent = the outfit hue, soft (saturation 35-55%, lightness 68-78%). Black/white/grey outfit: champagne `#CDB38A` on warm backgrounds, ice blue `#A9C7E8` on cool ones. Never bright red text.
- If the accent matches a big background area, change its lightness so it stands out.
- Say the chosen theme in one line ("Theme: ice blue + white, matched to your navy shirt").

## 3. Readability
- Captions must stand out clearly from the video behind them (strong contrast, about 4.5:1). Fix with an outline, shadow or a soft dark glow, never a box (unless the user asks for one).
- 90 px safe margin on all sides; keep clear of the bottom 18% and the right-side app buttons.
- At most 2 fonts per frame. Nothing overlapping or cut off.

## 4. Storyboard first (always)
Before building many videos: show the plan (hook, length, one line per clip) and 1 preview frame or a short sample. Build only what the user approves.

## 5. Music and sound (optional)
- Default: NO music. Offer it as an option.
- If they want music: render a 10-15 s preview first; keep it only if they approve. Music sits about 10 dB under the voice and dips under speech.
- Sound effects: few and soft (a light whoosh on a big change). Never cartoon sounds, never on every cut.

## 6. Heavy motion graphics (optional, say the cost)
Simple captions + zooms + proof screenshots = fast and cheap (recommended for most reels and ads).
Animated UI cards, 3D, Apple-style motion (Remotion / HyperFrames) = much slower and uses many more tokens. Offer it only for one hero video, and say so before starting.

## 7. When something is missing
- No name -> no name tag (say so in one line).
- Unclear word in the transcript -> best guess, list it at the end.
- No brand colour -> auto theme (section 2).
- No CTA -> ask; suggest 2-3 options from their offer.

## 8. Delivery checklist (before saying "done")
- [ ] Only the approved clips were built.
- [ ] The main person is on screen; no host face or voice.
- [ ] Nothing covers the face; captions readable; no wrong words (names, numbers, brands).
- [ ] First 2 seconds hook; the clip ends on the payoff.
- [ ] Loudness about -14 LUFS; no glitch frames.
- [ ] Ad end card clean; audience call-out/headline as agreed.
- [ ] File under ~30 MB if it will be sent on WhatsApp (re-encode if bigger).
- [ ] Tell the user what you checked and what you fixed.

## 9. Stay current
If `references/style-guide.md` "Last checked" is older than 4 weeks, do a quick web search for current short-form editing trends, use what fits, and add 2-3 lines there with the date.
